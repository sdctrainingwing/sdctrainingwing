HVAC Design & BIM Modeling (Mechanical Module)

Master HVAC sizing and 3D BIM modeling from scratch.

This module takes you from basic cooling concepts to advanced industrial HVAC design. You will first learn how to calculate heat loads manually using E-20 sheets and digitally with Carrier HAP software. Next, you will design duct networks to distribute air properly. Finally, you will move into Autodesk Revit to build 3D models, place equipment, and route ductwork. This course teaches you everything you need to transition smoothly from basic blueprints to real job-site engineering.
