Integrated MEP & BIM Master Program

Launch your career as an industry-ready MEP design & BIM specialist.

This complete master program takes you from basic engineering concepts to advanced project execution. You will learn how to design mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems, and then model them in 3D using Autodesk Revit. Guided by active industry consultants, you will work on real building projects. By the end of this course, you will have a professional portfolio and the practical skills to handle complex building designs from day one.
