Firefighting & Plumbing (PHE Module)

Design complete piping and building safety systems from basic to advanced.

Every modern building needs smart water management and fire safety systems. This combined module teaches you how to design these systems step by step. You will learn to calculate water demands, design clean water and drainage piping, and plan automated fire sprinkler networks. You will move from simple manual formulas straight into live 3D Revit models, learning how to place pipes, risers, and fixtures according to global safety standards.
