Frequently asked

Before you enroll

Answers to what people usually ask before signing up.

Who is this course designed for?

This program is open to fresh graduates, diploma holders, and working professionals in the Mechanical, Electrical, Civil, or Architecture fields who want to build a career in the design and engineering industry.

Do you offer a job placement guarantee?

No, we do not provide artificial placement guarantees. Instead, we focus on hard engineering competence. We train you directly on live consultant-level blueprints so you can clear interviews and manage projects confidently from your first day on the job.

What are the computer requirements for the classes?

To run the Revit software smoothly, you need a laptop or computer with a minimum of an Intel i5 or AMD Ryzen 5 processor, 16 GB of RAM, a 4 GB dedicated graphics card, and a 512 GB SSD storage drive.

What is the benefit of the Integrated Master Program over a single course?

Construction companies look for engineers who understand how all systems work together. The integrated course teaches you full multi-discipline coordination and clash detection using Navisworks, opening up four times as many job opportunities.

Are the classes recorded or live?

We provide 100% live interactive training sessions paired with intensive practical design and modeling lab work.
